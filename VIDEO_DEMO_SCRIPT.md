## 🎥 Recording Tips

1. **Use a real device** — emulator won't show MWA popups
2. **Have 2+ SOL** in devnet wallet for gas
3. **Pre-load a quest** so GPS matches your current location
4. **Keep Solana Explorer open** in split-screen to show TX confirmations
5. **Record at 1080p**, 30fps minimum
6. **Background music**: Subtle, upbeat, royalty-free
7. **Total duration**: Exactly 2:00 — juri sering skip video panjang
