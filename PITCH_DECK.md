# 🌍 EcoQuest - Pitch Deck

Berikut adalah konsep 3-slide deck (Pitch Deck) yang padat, jelas, dan memikat juri. Kamu bisa memindahkan konten ini ke Google Slides, Canva, atau Figma.

---

## Slide 1: The Problem & The Hook (Hooks the Judges)
**Headline:** Environmental Action is Broken. It's unverified, unrewarded, and easily faked.
**Visual Suggestion:** Split screen — left side shows a generic "I planted a tree" web form (easily faked), right side shows garbage in a river.

**Key Points (Bullet points):**
- **No Trust:** Current eco-apps rely on manual inputs. Users can easily fake GPS or upload stock photos to game the system.
- **No Incentive:** Volunteering is great, but without economic incentives, retention drops by 90% after the first month.
- **The "So What?":** We need a system where **real-world impact is cryptographically verified** and economically rewarded.

---

## Slide 2: The Solution (EcoQuest on Solana Seeker)
**Headline:** EcoQuest: Verifiable Regen-Finance (ReFi) powered by DePIN & Solana.
**Visual Suggestion:** A clean architecture diagram showing: User Action ➡️ Hardware GPS + Camera Proof ➡️ Solana Smart Contract ➡️ $SKR Tokens Earned.

**Key Points (Bullet points):**
- **Hardware-Backed Anti-Cheat:** We leverage Solana Seeker's hardware GPS and native AR camera to generate immutable Proof of Physical Activity. No more fake locations or photos.
- **Instant Rewards on Solana:** Verifications trigger instant minting of $SKR tokens via Anchor Smart Contracts. 400ms finality means immediate gratification.
- **Sustainable Tokenomics:** Users can Stake $SKR for 35% APY, delegate to Guardian Pool, use it for DAO Governance, battle in PvP Arenas, earn Eco Badges, or swap SKR ↔ SOL — creating multiple token sinks and sustained demand.
